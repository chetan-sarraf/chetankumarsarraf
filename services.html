Frequently Asked Questions (Birgunj & Nepal)

How much does interior design cost per sq. ft. in Birgunj, Nepal?

Pricing typically ranges from Rs. 25–45 per sq. ft. for 2D AutoCAD planning & MEP, Rs. 65–110 for complete 3D realistic rendering, and Rs. 1,600–2,800 per sq. ft. for full turnkey execution.

What is the advantage of hiring a Civil Engineer for interior design?

A Civil Engineer ensures all interior partitions, plumbing schematics, and ceiling loads are structurally sound. Furthermore, accurate Bill of Quantities (BOQ) estimation prevents contractor overcharging.

Do you provide 3D photorealistic renders and 360° virtual tours?

Yes, every client receives high-definition photorealistic 3D renders using SketchUp, Enscape, and V-Ray, along with interactive 360° virtual reality panoramas.

How long does the interior design and drafting process take?

Initial conceptual sketches are prepared within 3 to 5 business days. Full 3D visualizations and complete 2D AutoCAD execution blueprint sets are delivered within 10 to 14 business days.

Why should I invest in professional interior design instead of doing it myself?

Professional interior design helps optimize space utilization, improve functionality, avoid costly mistakes, and create a cohesive aesthetic that increases property value.

Why choose Scale and Spaces Pvt. Ltd. for interior design in Birgunj?

Scale and Spaces Pvt. Ltd. combines civil engineering expertise, innovative design solutions, realistic visualizations, transparent costing, and professional project coordination to deliver exceptional interiors tailored to your needs.
