Cookies Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 | Er. Chetan Kumar Sarraf (Birgunj, Nepal)

Welcome to https://chetankumarsarraf.com.np. This Cookies Policy explains how lightweight cookies and browser storage are used to maintain smooth UI/UX performance, save your design studio preferences, and support embedded engineering workflows.

1. Strictly Essential Cookies & Storage

These keys are required for essential website operation and security and remain active regardless of preference:

  • cks_cookie_consent_v1 — Stores your explicit privacy decision (Accepted, Declined, or Dismissed) so you are not prompted repeatedly.
  • UI Theme & Viewport State — Remembers Light/Dark mode preference and safe page state across views.

2. Functional Studio Preferences

Enabled when you click Accept on the privacy banner:

  • cks_gchat_history_v1 — Preserves your Chat Bot · Studio Desk conversation across refreshes.
  • cks_visitor_reviews_v1 — Retains your submitted client feedback draft across browsing sessions.
  • CAD & Graphics Preferences — Saves your selected architectural drawing layer, intensity setting, and adaptive device graphics profile.

3. Third-Party Embeds & Content Delivery

Certain interactive tools connect directly to trusted infrastructure providers when used:

  • Google Identity Services & Google Fonts — Powers optional Sign-In and fast web typography.
  • Google Maps & WhatsApp — Launches directions or direct consultation threads only when clicked by you.

4. Managing Cookies or Contacting the Studio

You can update your preference at any time using the buttons below or clear cookies in your browser settings. For privacy requests, contact Er. Chetan Kumar Sarraf via master.er.cks@outlook.com or +977 9766188838.
